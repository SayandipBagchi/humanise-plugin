# Criteria: humanise-core-rewrite

Score each item independently. An item that cannot be checked from the output is a fail, not a pass.

- [ ] Contains no em dash, en dash, spaced hyphen used as a dash, or double hyphen
- [ ] Contains no not-X-but-Y construction in any form, including 'not just ... it's'
- [ ] Does not end on a one-line closer that restates the paragraph above it
- [ ] Preserves all four supported facts: 9 seconds, 40 seconds, 50,000 rows, timeout gone, progress bar shows a row count
- [ ] Preserves the open item about scheduled exports without inflating or resolving it
- [ ] Invents no number, percentage, user outcome, adoption claim, or team result
- [ ] Removes the emoji-free title-case heading style, the horizontal rule, and the bold label on every list item, or converts the list to prose
- [ ] Removes 'pivotal moment', 'underscoring our commitment', 'data-driven culture', and 'the future looks bright'
- [ ] Opens with content rather than a staged run-up such as 'Let's dive into' or 'Here's what shipped'
- [ ] Response carries no assistant wrapper: no 'Here is', no praise, no offer to continue, no claim that the result is now human
- [ ] Output length is between 85% and 115% of the input, or the response names the cut and why
